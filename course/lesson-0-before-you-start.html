Before you start

What the test version is, what it keeps, and who does what in VersionPoint.

Why it matters

The test version is a real copy of VersionPoint, but it keeps everything in your browser and has no sign-in. Knowing that up front keeps real documents out of it, and keeps your practice from disappearing.

Watch

Who does what

VersionPoint is for one comms person on one computer: you. Two kinds of people take part in a document's review.

WhoWhat they do
YouEverything: import the draft, send each review copy, bring it back, approve or reject every edit, and make the copies that go out. Your name goes on the copies you send and the decisions you make.
Your reviewersRead the Word file you send them, edit it with Track Changes, and send it back. They never sign in to VersionPoint and never see it.

In the sample you are Sam Sample, the one person in the Office of Example Affairs (sample). The one person is always the office's Admin, which is why Settings is in the bar.

What the test version keeps, and where

Everything you do is kept in this browser, on this computer: the documents, every version, round, decision and note, and the log under Settings. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no sign-in.

To keep a copy of a document and its whole record, download its Project file (lesson 8). It opens again with Open a project file on Documents, here or on another computer.

Try it

  1. Open the VersionPoint test version in a second window, beside this page.
  2. Read the banner under the lead line. Then, under Try it with a sample, choose Open the sample.
  3. On Documents, find Riverside Trail opening (sample) and its tag, 2 imported changes to decide. Don't open it yet.
  4. Look at the right end of the bar at the top: Sam Sample, with Office of Example Affairs (sample) under it.
  5. Choose Settings. Find License, Activity and Start over. Don't choose anything under Start over.
  6. Choose Documents in the bar at the top to go back.

Check your work

Watch out for

Documents live in this browser only. A different browser or computer starts empty, and clearing your browser's data clears them too. To keep a backup of a document and its record, or move it to another computer, download its Project file (lesson 8).
Never put a real draft into the test version, even though nothing is sent anywhere. Use the course's practice release or something already public.
Clear everything in this browser can't be undone. It's under Start over on Settings, and it clears the office and every document. Leave it alone until you've finished the course.
