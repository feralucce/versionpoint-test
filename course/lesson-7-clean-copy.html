Lesson 78 minutesThe Lake Shuttle, version 2

Send a clean copy

Check the copy that leaves the office, fix what the check warns about with Edit it yourself, and download the clean copy.

Why it matters

The copy that leaves the office (to the press shop, a partner, the web team) has to carry the words and nothing else. VersionPoint checks every copy before you can download it, and warns about text that looks like a placeholder, like the [insert year] you let through in lesson 5.

Watch

What the check stops

If a copy ever held a margin note, a tracked change, hidden text, a name in its properties or data left by another system, the check would list it under The copy didn't pass the check, so it can't be downloaded: and keep Download the clean copy greyed out.

You won't see that with the practice release. VersionPoint builds every version without those things, so the check is a last guard, not a cleanup step. It's there so that a reviewer's comment never rides out with a release, whatever happened to the file on the way.

The warnings are different: they're about the words. The check looks for text Word and drafters leave behind, like Click or tap here to enter text, Lorem ipsum, TK, XXX, or anything in square brackets that starts with insert, add, link or TBD. It points them out and leaves the decision to you.

Fix it with "Edit it yourself"

Don't fix the year in the downloaded clean copy: that change would never reach VersionPoint or the record. Make it the same way a reviewer would, as a round of your own.

Try it

Start on the document page of Lake Shuttle news release, version 2, where lesson 6 left you. In step 4 you can fill in the year in Word, or use the reviewer's desk to do it for you.

Practice files
  • The reviewer's desk: plays the reviewer on a copy you downloaded.
  1. Choose Clean copy. Read the check: it passes, and under Worth a look before it goes out: it asks whether [insert year] in paragraph 8 is meant to be there. Don't download this one.
  2. Choose Close, then Edit it yourself, then Make the copy. Lake Shuttle news release - edit copy - round 2.docx downloads, and the form says Make your changes in Word. Track Changes is already on and locked, so every edit is recorded. When you're done, bring the file back here.
  3. Choose Done. The banner says Edit copy out with Sam Sample, with today's date and round 2.
  4. Fill in the year. In Word: open the edit copy, replace [insert year] with 2028, and save. Or on the reviewer's desk: choose the edit copy, then choose Download the returned file.
  5. Back on the document page, choose Bring back the returned file, and in Bring back the edit copy choose your edited copy. Round 2: own edits by Sam Sample opens, with one card: [insert year] changed to 2028.
  6. Choose Accept on card 1, then Finish. The version list now says Version 3 (latest): Own edits by Sam Sample.
  7. Choose Clean copy again. It passes, and this time there's no Worth a look.
  8. Choose Download the clean copy. The form adds Downloaded: Lake Shuttle news release.docx. Choose Close.

Check your work

Watch out for

Warnings don't stop the download. The copy passed the check. means nothing hidden is in the file. A placeholder in the words is a different problem, and only you can catch it. Read the Worth a look list every time.
Paragraph numbers count every line from the top. NEWS RELEASE is paragraph 1, so Paragraph 8 is the eighth line of the release, not the eighth paragraph of body text.
Never fix the clean copy in Word. That change lives only in your downloaded file: the next clean copy, the record and the next reviewer would all still have the old words. Fix it with Edit it yourself.
Clean copy gives the last approved version. It's there while a round is out or waiting for decisions, and it leaves out the edits still waiting for you. The form says so: the latest approved version.
