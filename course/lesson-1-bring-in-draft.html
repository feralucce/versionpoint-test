Lesson 16 minutesThe Lake Shuttle

Bring in a draft

Import a Word draft, give it a real title, and read its document page.

Why it matters

VersionPoint keeps the draft exactly as it came in, letterhead and styles included, and every later version is built on it. Bring it in once, and the Draft1, Draft2 files in your inbox stop.

Watch

A draft that already has edits

Program offices often send a draft with their tracked changes and comments still in it. Bring it in as it is. The app's own sample release came in that way:

Try it

Download the practice draft. It's a one-page made-up release, The Lake Shuttle, that you'll use in every lesson from here on.

Practice files
  • Lake Shuttle draft.docx
  1. Download Lake Shuttle draft.docx from the practice files above. Leave it where your browser saves downloads.
  2. In the test version, on Documents, choose Import a document.
  3. Choose the file box, then choose Lake Shuttle draft.docx. The box shows the file name and 4 KB. Click to choose a different file., and Title fills in as Lake Shuttle draft.
  4. Choose Import. The document page opens. The version list shows Version 1 (latest): Imported, and the line beside it says you imported it today.
  5. Choose Rename beside the title. Replace the title with Lake Shuttle news release, then choose Save. The new title shows at the top.
  6. Look at Rounds on the right: one round, Imported: Lake Shuttle draft.docx, No changes.
  7. Choose ← Documents to go back to the list.

Check your work

Watch out for

Old .doc files won't import. The app says: VersionPoint imports Word files (.docx) and PDFs. Older .doc files: open them in Word and save as .docx first.
The title starts as the file name. Rename it to what the document is before you send it, because the title names every copy: Lake Shuttle news release - for Riley Planner - round 1.docx reads better in a reviewer's inbox than Lake Shuttle draft - for Riley Planner - round 1.docx.
A PDF comes in as text only. Its layout can't come across, so import the Word file whenever you have it.
