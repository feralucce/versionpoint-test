Working as a team

Add a colleague to a document, pass it on, approve an executive's suggested edit, and take the whole record with you in a project file.

Why it matters

Documents change hands. People go on leave, Leads pick things up, and executives want a say. VersionPoint keeps one person in charge, lets others help, and makes sure an executive's edit is decided like anyone else's, with the decision in the record.

An executive's suggested edits

An Executive can open any document but can't send it, decide on it or import anything. What they can do is suggest edits, and every one of them waits for the comms team.

When someone leaves

If the person in charge leaves, an Admin marks them Has left on the Office page. Nothing is lost: their documents say Sam Sample is no longer in the office. The document and its record are all here., and the Admin's page lists every document that needs someone new in charge. An Admin or a Lead then uses Pass it on. Back again undoes it. The add-on course for Admins walks through it.

The project file

Project file on the document page downloads the document with its whole record: Lake Shuttle news release.vpproj. On another computer, Open a project file on Documents brings it in. Its form says: A project file (.vpproj) holds a document with its whole record: versions, rounds, decisions and notes. Someone on the team can pass one to you until the office's SharePoint library is connected.

Try it

Start on the document page of Lake Shuttle news release, version 3, as Sam Sample. You'll switch people with Viewing as at the top; in your agency's VersionPoint each person signs in as themselves. Lee Leader's edit comes from the reviewer's desk, which plays Lee when you give it Lee's copy.

Practice files
  • The reviewer's desk: plays the reviewer on a copy you downloaded.
  1. In Viewing as, choose Ana Other: Member. Documents says No documents yet: Ana isn't on the release.
  2. Switch back to Sam Sample: Member and open the release. Choose Choose people, tick Ana Other, and choose Save. Also working on it now says Ana Other.
  3. Choose Pass it on and look at the form, then choose Cancel. You stay in charge.
  4. Switch to Ana Other: Member and choose Notifications. It says Sam Sample added you to the release, and the release is in Ana's list. Choose Notifications again to close it.
  5. Switch to Lee Leader: Executive, open the release, choose Suggest edits, then Make the copy, then Done. The banner says Lee Leader is suggesting edits, with the date and round 3.
  6. On the reviewer's desk, choose Lee's copy, Lake Shuttle news release - edits by Lee Leader - round 3.docx, then choose Download the returned file.
  7. Back on the release, still as Lee, choose Bring back your edits and choose Lee's returned file. Round 3 opens with one card and no Accept or Reject. Choose ← Lake Shuttle news release: the banner says Revisions are waiting for the comms team's approval.
  8. Switch to Ana Other: Member. On Documents, the release is tagged 1 executive edit to approve, and Notifications has Lee Leader suggested 1 edit. Switch to Hana Hub: Lead and choose Notifications: Hana has it too.
  9. Switch to Sam Sample: Member, open the release and choose Decide revisions. On card 1 (Families told us becomes Riverton families told us), choose Accept, then Finish. The version list says Version 4 (latest): Edits by Lee Leader (executive), approved.
  10. Switch to Lee Leader: Executive and choose Notifications. It says Lee's suggested edits were decided: 1 of 1 accepted.
  11. Switch back to Sam Sample: Member, open the release and choose Project file. Lake Shuttle news release.vpproj downloads. Keep it: it's your backup of everything so far.

Check your work

Watch out for

Executive edits go to the comms team, not to one approver. The person in charge, the people added to the document and every Lead are told, and any of them can approve. Agree in the office who decides, so each one doesn't wait for the others.
People you add can work, not manage. Ana can send, bring back and decide, but only the person in charge, a Lead or an Admin sees Pass it on and Choose people.
Opening a project file you already have is turned away: You already have this document, as recent as this file or more, with the date it was saved. Nothing is overwritten.
In the test version, a project file is the only way to move a document to another browser or computer. Everything else stays in the browser you used.
