The reviewer's desk

Your reviewers work in Word. Here, the course plays them: drop in the copy you downloaded from VersionPoint, and the reviewer makes their edits and notes on it, the way Word would. Then bring the file back in VersionPoint.

Nothing leaves this computer.

The file is changed in this browser and handed straight back to you. It only works on the course's practice release, The Lake Shuttle.
