Lesson 86 minutesThe Lake Shuttle, version 3

The record

Answer why does this line read this way? from the paragraph history, older versions and the record.

Why it matters

Sooner or later someone asks why a line reads the way it does. The record answers with names, dates, the reviewer's note and your reason, without anyone digging through email.

Watch

At the end, Notes added in VersionPoint lists the notes and replies made in the app, like your reply on the grant note and your note on Saturdays and Sundays.

Try it

Start on the document page of Lake Shuttle news release, version 3, where lesson 7 left you.

  1. Click the paragraph about the buses (The shuttle uses four buses…). How this paragraph got here opens, with Riley's two revisions in version 2, your reason on the rejected one, and both of Riley's notes.
  2. Choose Close, then click the first paragraph (the schedule). Under version 2, find 28 → 27 and made without Track Changes.
  3. Choose Close. In the version list, choose Version 1: Imported. The banner says You're looking at version 1 of 3: Imported. Choose Back to the latest.
  4. Under Rounds, choose Round 1: Riley Planner. The round opens read-only, with decided by Sam Sample on each card. Choose ← Lake Shuttle news release to go back.
  5. Choose Record. Find the Versions table (three versions), then round 1's table, with Rejected, Sam Sample and your reason on revision 4.
  6. Choose Print or save as PDF if you'd like a copy. Your browser's print window opens; cancel it or save the PDF.

Check your work

Watch out for

The record is internal. It holds every margin note and reason, and says so at the top. Don't send it, or a PDF of it, outside the office; send the clean copy.
Print or save as PDF is your browser's own print window, so how it looks depends on your browser and printer. Save as PDF when records staff want a file.
Notes and replies you add in VersionPoint are listed together under Notes added in VersionPoint. A reply doesn't say which note it answers, so word replies so they stand on their own.

Quick check
